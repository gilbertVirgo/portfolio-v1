Project links at tablet width

Problem: at 768 the meta row has room for two columns, so the links fall to a row of their own and sit right-aligned with nothing above them (Christian Heritage London), or wrap inside a 200px column (the rest). Five treatments below, each on the two-link case and on a card with a note instead of a link. All links open in a new tab.
